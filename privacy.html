Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 14, 2026

1. About Bhor Analytics

Bhor Analytics is a private-use application developed to collect and analyze performance data for the BHOR YouTube channel. It is intended for use by the channel owner and is not offered as a public analytics service.

2. Information accessed

With authorization, the application accesses YouTube channel information, video metadata, channel and video performance statistics, traffic source reports, and audience retention information through Google and YouTube APIs.

The application also uses Google Drive access to store and retrieve its own analytics backups.

3. How information is used

Information is used for historical analytics, performance comparisons, reporting, troubleshooting, and understanding the performance of the BHOR YouTube channel.

4. Data storage and security

Structured analytics data is stored in a private DuckDB database. Raw API responses and database backups are maintained using local storage and an access-controlled Google Drive account.

Authentication credentials are kept outside the public website and are not intentionally published in the project's source repository.

5. Data sharing

Bhor Analytics is not designed to sell or publicly distribute the Google user data it accesses. Data is processed within the owner's analytics infrastructure and stored using Google Drive for backup purposes.

6. Retention and deletion

Analytics records and backups are retained for historical analysis. The owner can delete locally stored records and Google Drive backups when they are no longer required. No automatic retention or deletion schedule is currently implemented.

7. Revoking access

Google account authorization can be revoked through the account's third-party connection settings. Revoking access prevents further authorized API access but does not automatically delete previously stored data.

8. Third-party services

The application uses Google and YouTube APIs and Google Drive. Use of YouTube API Services is also subject to the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy .

9. Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated if the application's data handling practices or functionality change. The latest version will be published here.

10. Contact

For privacy-related questions, contact bhorrecordsindia@gmail.com .
